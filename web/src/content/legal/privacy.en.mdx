D Deal Inspector & Interior respects your privacy. This page explains how we collect, use and protect your personal data.

> Draft document. The structure and headings follow PDPA guidelines, and the details are being reviewed by the responsible party before this policy takes effect.

## 1. Data we collect

When you contact us to ask about or book a service, we collect only the information needed to provide it: your name, phone number, email address, the address or location of the property to be inspected, and the date and time of your appointment.

If you contact us through LINE, Facebook or another social platform, the account details you use will be visible to us according to that platform's settings.

## 2. How we use your data

We use your data to schedule and book inspections, coordinate with you before and after the visit, prepare and deliver your inspection report, and answer questions about our services.

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties for marketing.

## 3. Legal basis

We process your data on the basis of performing the service contract you requested and/or the consent you gave when you first contacted us.

## 4. Disclosure

We may share your data, only as far as necessary, with our team and the engineers carrying out your inspection, and with government authorities where the law requires it.

## 5. Retention

We keep your data only for as long as necessary for the purposes above and for any period required by applicable law. Once it is no longer needed, we delete it or make it non-identifiable.

## 6. Your rights

Under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), you have the right to access your data, obtain a copy, have it corrected, have it deleted, restrict its use, object to processing, and withdraw your consent. You also have the right to file a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Committee.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details at the end of this page.

## 7. Cookies and analytics

This website may use cookies that are essential for basic page functionality. If we add analytics tools in the future, we will update this policy and let you know.
